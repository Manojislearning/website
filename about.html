About The Manospace Architects

A Bengaluru-based architecture and BIM practice specializing in design, documentation, and digital workflows.

Who We Are

The Manospace Architects is a Bengaluru-based architecture and BIM-driven design practice founded by Manoj Yadav. We specialize in architectural design, façade BIM modeling, interiors, landscape design, and construction documentation.

Our practice combines practical architectural expertise with advanced BIM workflows, focusing on accuracy, coordination, and execution-ready documentation. We work with architects, builders, façade consultants, and construction teams to deliver projects with precision and clarity.

Our Approach

We believe in designing with purpose and documenting with precision. Our work is guided by three core principles:

Design Excellence

Architecture that responds to site, context, and user needs with thoughtful design decisions.

BIM Excellence

Accurate, coordinated models that enable better planning, quantity control, and execution.

Collaboration

Clear communication and seamless coordination with builders, consultants, and contractors.

Specialization

Our practice has developed specialized expertise in several areas:

  • Glass Façade BIM Modeling: LOD 400 parametric models with panel numbering, elevations, and quantity schedules
  • Architectural BIM: Complete building models with coordination and clash detection
  • Dynamo Automation: Scripted workflows for repetitive tasks and parametric design
  • Shop Drawings & Documentation: Construction-ready drawings and quantity take-offs
  • Interior & Landscape Design: Conceptual to execution-ready documentation

Principal Architect

Manoj Yadav is a passionate architect and BIM enthusiast with extensive experience in architectural design, Revit modeling, and digital workflows. Manoj has worked on diverse projects ranging from residential and commercial spaces to specialized glass façade systems and interior renovations.
