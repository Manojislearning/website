BIM Standards & LOD

Industry-Standard Levels of Detail

LOD 200

Approximate geometry and properties. Suitable for conceptual design and feasibility studies.

LOD 300

Defined geometry and properties. Suitable for design development and planning.

LOD 350

Precise geometry with connections. Suitable for detailed design and coordination.

LOD 400

Complete assembly ready for fabrication. Includes all specifications and shop drawings.
